# Campaign Audit

## Summary

The current playable campaign keeps 64 of 84 authored levels. The first chapter is now an
11-level free preview that introduces basic sequencing, early process reuse, nested
helpers, a compact process-planning capstone, and one switch-state teaser before the premium height chapter. The
later recursive/decoy chapters are compressed into a shorter advanced run, followed by a
five-level advanced switchback chapter and a seven-level large landscape chapter.

Status meanings:

- Keep: stays in the current playable campaign.
- Cut: remains authored content, but is removed from the current campaign order.
- Revise later: keep the idea under review before adding it back.

## Follow-Up

The cut levels should stay in source for now. They are useful as a design bank and regression
coverage, but they should not define the first complete playthrough until manual playtesting
shows that the advanced campaign needs more length.
